## Capture notes

- Captured in Google Chrome. Mobile means responsive web at approximately 390 px wide, not native iOS/Android screens. Site zoom and AI output can change image dimensions.
- The user authorized a neutral sample image in unpublished drafts. Nothing was posted, shared, scheduled, broadcast or exported. Video upload was not used.
- Some Facebook mobile destinations show their navigation column with content offscreen. TikTok Studio retains a wide editor layout. VEED mobile uses bottom-sheet drawers. These limitations are recorded on the corresponding cards and in per-app coverage reports.
- X Grok's desktop account-linking prompt was captured without accepting its agreement. X Money shows the mobile-app handoff only. X Ads was not opened: automatic approval review blocked the separate private advertising dashboard. Its entry appears in the More menu.
- External advertising, payments, and separate Meta/Google products, nested settings, and further compose options are outside the final reduced scope.
- Darwin account references are removed with the built-in AI image editor when needed, following the user's preference. Edited images are labeled; they are visual references and may have incidental differences. Raw originals and excluded intermediate captures remain outside this collection.
