## Source and visible evidence

Dub documents public analytics pages for individual links and link folders, intended for sharing results with clients or external stakeholders. This capture is one such public report. [Source](https://dub.co/help/article/share-analytics).

- Click count, a time-series chart, and date/filter controls.
- Referrer and UTM sections.
- Geographic, device, browser, and operating-system breakdown tabs.
