## Source and visible evidence

The official kit includes a payments app. Its visible money and card controls are demo UI; payment execution was not tested. [Source](https://www.shadcnblocks.com/template/admin).

- Pending and settled balances, gross volume, and net revenue.
- Payment volume, method mix, and dispute-health panels.
- A personal/corporate card section and recent checkout, subscription, and refund entries.
