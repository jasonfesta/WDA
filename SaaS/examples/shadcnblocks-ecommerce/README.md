## Source and visible evidence

The official kit lists ecommerce among its six app types and identifies Next.js, shadcn/ui, and Tailwind as its foundation. This capture does not establish a connected store backend. [Source](https://www.shadcnblocks.com/template/admin).

- Monthly revenue, fulfilled orders, new customers, and refunds.
- Total Revenue and Revenue by Channel panels.
- Average order value, average sales, and product-category panels.
