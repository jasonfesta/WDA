## Counting and evidence rules

- One distinct captured app view is one example. Different views of the same project may count separately; themes do not.
- Repository search results, open tabs, browser previews, and inspection-only screenshots are not saved examples.
- The 129 repository search results are a candidate pool. Twenty repositories received deeper metadata/README review; the first 15 in the research table are a star-based shortlist, not a verified global usage ranking.
- Three of those top-15 projects have saved examples: AdminLTE, Tabler, and Shadcn Admin. The collection does not claim to capture all top 15.
- Source facts and observed interface features are distinguished from inferred uses in each example README. Screenshots do not verify production backends.
- [Capture manifest](captures.json) records source URLs, actual image dimensions, byte sizes, and SHA-256 hashes.
