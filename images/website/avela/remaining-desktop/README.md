Each companion distinguishes observed live content from AVELA proposals. Some checkout and confirmation states are described by official service documentation rather than an exercised competitor transaction; the companions disclose this limit. No competitor form or checkout was submitted.

Privacy and Terms are shopper-facing draft specimens for layout review. Their seller/controller details, jurisdiction, policies, providers and actual practices still require confirmation before publication. Shipping windows, prices for delivery, repair charges and commitments have not been invented. Sample receipts represent design states only.

The original homepage footer is retained as supplied artwork. Its available raster detail is lower than the newly generated bodies; it is proportionately resized without changing its words or layout. Journal and article include the original blue newsletter panel. Other pages use the footer alone. New mobile layouts are outside this desktop review pass.
