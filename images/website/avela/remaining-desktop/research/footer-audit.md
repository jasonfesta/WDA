## Shared elements and boundaries

The main header remains AVELA / The bags / Our workshop / Care & repair / Bag. The count reflects the depicted cart state. Footer artwork and its link groups are assembled from the original homepage. Newsletter artwork is reused only where an editorial close suits the page. No new site implementation, policy publication, checkout, contact submission or repair request is performed.

Images are design review artifacts. Business facts needed before a live launch are recorded in the relevant companions rather than invented in the images. Privacy and Terms use one consistent reading template; they are not final legal advice or operational policy.
