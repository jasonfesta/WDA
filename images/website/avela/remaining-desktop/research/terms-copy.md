## 01 / About these terms

The final terms will identify the seller and the markets covered. They will explain how these terms apply when you use the website and purchase an AVELA product.

## 02 / Products and availability

Product descriptions, selected colours and availability should be clear before purchase. The published terms will explain how differences in screen display, stock changes and product information are handled.

## 03 / Orders and payment

Review your chosen items and the full amount before payment. The final terms will explain the steps for placing an order, when it is accepted and when payment is taken.

## 04 / Prices and delivery

Currency, taxes, shipping costs and delivery information will be explained before an order is placed. The published terms will set out the destinations served and the process for delivery questions.

## 05 / Returns and cancellations

The final policy will state eligibility, time limits, return costs, cancellation options and refund steps. These details will reflect the consumer rights and practices that apply in each selling market.

## 06 / Care and repairs

Care guidance and repair requests have separate service information. A request is not confirmation of a repair. Any proposed work, cost and timing should be reviewed before agreement.

## 07 / Website use and your rights

The final terms will explain permitted website use, content ownership and the applicable rights and dispute process. Verified contact details and an effective date will be added before publication.
