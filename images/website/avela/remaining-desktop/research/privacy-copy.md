# Privacy — design draft copy

Draft for review

## 01 / About this notice

This notice will explain how personal information is handled when you browse AVELA, place an order or contact us. The published version will identify the organisation responsible and how to reach its privacy contact.

## 02 / Information and purpose

The final notice will describe the information collected through shopping, enquiries and newsletter requests, together with the purpose and applicable legal basis for each use. It will distinguish required information from optional details.

## 03 / Service providers and transfers

The published notice will identify relevant recipient categories, service providers and any transfers across borders. It will explain the safeguards that apply to those arrangements.

## 04 / Retention and security

The final notice will explain how retention periods are determined and describe the safeguards used to protect personal information. These details must reflect AVELA's actual practices.

## 05 / Your choices and rights

The published notice will explain the rights and choices available in each market, how to make a request and how to raise a privacy concern. Any identity checks and response process will be stated clearly.

## 06 / Cookies and similar technologies

The final notice will describe the technologies actually used, their purposes and durations, and any consent or preference controls available. Optional technologies must match the choices offered on the site.

## 07 / Contact and updates

Verified contact details and the effective date will be added before publication. Material changes and the route for privacy questions will be explained here.
