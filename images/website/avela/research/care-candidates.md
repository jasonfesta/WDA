# Care references — demonstrative candidates

21 October 2026. Official primary pages reopened. Research only; no images generated. Source text and media labels verified here. Root agent is inspecting live Chrome presentation and playback.

## Recommendation

Use **Jason Markk’s Celine Belt Bag tutorial** as the principal visual companion for an item-led AVELA care lesson. It is the closest literal match to showing hands performing a short sequence on one identifiable bag. Keep the Arc visible and consistent through every proposed instructional image.

**Gap:** none of these verified pages teaches both cleaning and structural repair on one named bag. Do not imply otherwise. A repair-help route would be an explicitly authored AVELA addition; Coach is the strongest alternative if keeping care and repair resources in one hub matters more than named-model specificity.

## 1. Jason Markk — strongest single-item demonstration

[Official tutorial](https://jasonmarkk.com/blogs/videos-tutorials/how-to-clean-leather-handbags)

Observed sequence: tutorial title and hero image → concise introduction identifying the **Celine Belt Bag** → featured products / four numbered actions → embedded video → merchandise promotions. The four action labels are “Dry Brush”, “Apply Foam”, “Brush And Wipe Clean”, and “Apply Balm Over Entire Surface”.

**Adapt:** one named bag, one uninterrupted demonstration, short action captions. Exclude the large commercial tail.

**Limits:** a care-product maker’s tutorial, not Celine-issued guidance. AVELA finish, dye and product compatibility remain unverified; transfer the instructional format, not chemical treatments or performance claims. Structural repair is absent.

## 2. Coach — strongest manufacturer care-and-repair hub

[Official Product Care page](https://www.coach.com/support/product-care)

Observed sequence: introduction → illustrated material choices → core-leather home-care video → specific material guidance → support and expert-video links for stain treatment, storage and hardware cleaning/replacement. Separate specialty-leather treatment prevents a universal routine.

**Adapt:** show the chosen AVELA bag first, demonstrate its relevant care, then offer clearly named help.

**Limits:** broad material hub rather than one named model. Coach cleaners, retail services, lifetime-care language, leather certifications and specialty instructions do not transfer. The linked hardware video is [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UcwxzsI4y58); official link verified, playback not inspected by this agent.

## 3. Bellroy — clearest numbered instructions plus video

[Official cleaning and care](https://bellroy.com/customer-care/cleaning-and-care)

Observed sequence: fabric cleaning steps and video → leather section with moisture response, three cleaner steps, three conditioner steps and another video → luggage cleaning → support. Different product materials receive distinct routines.

**Adapt:** pair a compact numbered procedure with an accessible demonstration video and an ordinary support link.

**Limits:** product-type guidance, not a single handbag model. No structural-repair lesson. Fabric detergents and leather conditioners must never be merged into one routine. AVELA compatibility must be established independently.

## Selection and design implications

The desired lesson should make the physical action visible: tool in hand, exact bag area, concise instruction and relevant result. A decorative leather macro does not demonstrate care. For a static image study, use stills with visible hand/tool action and an honest video-play affordance; do not represent invented media as a working video.
