## Principal reference

[Jason Markk — leather-handbag tutorial](https://jasonmarkk.com/blogs/videos-tutorials/how-to-clean-leather-handbags)

The official tutorial identifies a Celine Belt Bag, introduces a cleaning-and-conditioning demonstration, lists four short actions and includes video. The useful pattern is a recognisable item undergoing visible, ordered actions. This source does **not** teach structural repair. The live page was inspected in Chrome.

Supporting source: [Coach — Product Care](https://www.coach.com/support/product-care). Coach separates guidance by material and provides support and expert-video routes, including hardware help. This supports differentiating care by bag/finish and offering professional assistance. Coach products, service promises and technical treatment instructions do not transfer.

## Proposed production interactions

These are implementation specifications for a future site, not claims that the image mockups function.

**Bag selection.** Arc is the initial selected state. Each bag choice visibly updates the selected label, hero poster, relevant care content and repair-form model. Preserve normal Back behavior and expose selection to assistive technology. Only display a model-specific routine after the underlying material guidance has been approved; avoid implying that changing colour alone guarantees identical care.

**Watch and read.** The hero play control opens an accessible player with pause, volume, captions and fullscreen controls. Do not autoplay audio. Provide a nearby transcript link and a playable-media error state that leaves written steps available. Captions describe speech and necessary non-speech cues. Visible chapters correspond to the three care steps; choosing one seeks the proposed video to that chapter and visibly identifies it. Without a loaded video, chapters can navigate to the written section.

**Steps and support.** Each photograph depicts the action being described; each caption remains readable without playing video. Mobile keeps the same sequence and places the caption directly beside its image in reading order. A care-question link carries the selected model into Contact without creating a repair request.

**Repair intake.** Proposed fields: Model, Concern, What have you noticed?, Photographs, Name and Email; Order number is optional. Model and concern offer Not sure / Other. Photo guidance asks for a full-bag view and a close-up, with a contact alternative for people unable to attach an image. Display permanent field labels and distinguish required from optional fields. Accepted formats and file limits must be set by the eventual upload service.

**Review and recovery.** Selected attachments show a thumbnail, filename, upload status and Remove action. Invalid fields receive specific messages and a linked error summary; preserve valid entries. Review shows the selected bag, concern, photo count and contact details with Edit controls. A send action is separate from review. Show a receipt only after confirmed service acknowledgement; an unknown outcome retains the draft and checks the original request before inviting a repeat. Do not imply a repair is approved or ask the customer to mail a bag without instructions.

**Newsletter.** Submit explicitly. Validate email, retain input on failure, and show success only after acknowledgement. Newsletter subscription is separate from repair/contact consent.
