## Approved direction

Workshop opens with a large film poster, followed by an oversized Arc with detail hotspots and a rotation grip, two alternating image-and-text sections, and lifestyle imagery. Its one-to-one editorial companion is [HEREU Craftsmanship](https://hereustudio.com/pages/craftmanship); the 3D showcase follows the approved AVELA brief.

Care & repair follows the Arc through a demonstration, three illustrated care habits and a loose-stitch assessment example. Its principal companion is [Jason Markk’s tutorial using a Celine Belt Bag](https://jasonmarkk.com/blogs/videos-tutorials/how-to-clean-leather-handbags), supported by [Coach’s material-specific care guidance](https://www.coach.com/support/product-care). The companion distinguishes observed source content from AVELA proposals.

Both new pages include the original homepage newsletter and footer, reused as device-specific artwork. Bags retains its separately approved catalogue layout and footer. All mobile layouts are individually composed and exported at 390 px wide; heights follow content.

The images were made with built-in image generation and assembled proportionally. Desktop files preserve the narrowest generated section’s native width; mobile masters preserve native section detail, with separate 390 px review exports. The original newsletter/footer have the detail of the supplied homepage raster. Video, 3D, filtering, care and repair interactions are depicted design states; the companions record the intended future behavior. Material guidance and service terms require confirmation against the actual product before implementation.

Earlier rejected page drafts are not part of this approved collection.
