Ordering & booking — Version 2 image studies

16 original raster studies for Supper, Fieldwork, Wayfar and Stillhouse.
Each brand includes desktop website, mobile website, native app showcase
and component. These are image studies in a static review gallery.

Open original-studies-v2/index.html through a static server. From the
repository root, run:

  python3 -m http.server 5194 --directory output

Then visit:
  http://127.0.0.1:5194/hd-04-ordering-booking/original-studies-v2/

Use Show reference to compare each brand with its source screenshots.
Click an image to inspect it at a larger size.

Included:
- original-studies-v2/: 16 final images, HTML viewer, prompts, provenance,
  asset hashes and desktop/mobile review screenshots.
- market-references/: approved DoorDash, Instacart, KAYAK and Booking.com
  website captures and official publisher app previews, plus the shared
  gallery stylesheet used by Version 2.
- visual-studies-v1/: preserved earlier image studies and viewer.
- flow-research/research-and-screen-plan.txt: earlier flow plan linked
  from the Version 1 viewer.

The rejected editorial prompts are retained as historical records in
original-studies-v2/desktop-prompts.json. Their outputs are not used.
Final generation prompts are in original-studies-v2/prompts.json.

This set covers four reference areas per brand. It does not complete all
states in the earlier 32-screen sequential-flow plan. No product UI,
orders, payments or bookings were implemented or completed.
