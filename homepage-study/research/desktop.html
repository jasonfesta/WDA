Desktop reference research

Jason Festa · WDA / Homepage study · 21 October 2026

Five curated references per category. These are design references, not a claim of a popularity ranking. The live-reference register distinguishes inspected interactions from proposed behavior.

Avela / Soft Form

Terracotta and sea-blue fashion editorial with oxblood leather. Broad cream display letters coexist with delicate serif copy. Let cream product fields alternate with confident terracotta, blue and dark wine sections.

Polène — Campaign to category links, compact bestsellers, paired campaign imagery, editorial story, stores, Instagram, newsletter and service footer. Visual sample: full-width paired editorial photographs separated from product grids by generous space.

Hereu — Material and handwork photography supports the maker story; bags grouped by useful form and carrying category.

Neous — Sculptural form is connected to everyday function, craft, materials and ownership support. Do not transfer their certifications or repair guarantees.

Cuyana — Bag families, organizers and carrying systems connect aspirational photography with use; materials and care navigation. Dynamic homepage extraction was partial.

Mansur Gavriel — Campaign-led category entry, distinct bag families and a simple care, resale and newsletter footer.

Room & Ritual

Rosette pink, deep burgundy, olive velvet, walnut, wool and sculptural orange light. Heavy wordmark plus graceful serif storytelling. Precisely aligned rectangular photography; warm homes with actual inhabitation.

Norm Architects — Project chapters connect residential and hospitality work to furniture, objects and publications. Content reviewed; some images failed to load in the sampled browser viewport.

Studio Ashby — Regular three-column rectangular interior photographs with simple project captions. Visually inspected: artwork, textiles and lived-in furnishings give each project identity.

Studioilse — Homes, public spaces, things and strategy framed around human use. Transfer the connection between objects and daily rituals.

Workstead — Clear routes for buildings/interiors and lighting; project inquiry and tailored newsletter interests.

Roman and Williams — Buildings, furniture, lighting, makers and hospitality form an interconnected editorial world.

Oriel

Monumental cream lowercase type over rust earth architecture, precise daylight, curved honey oak and brass. Lower page uses a disciplined architectural grid and generous image-led case studies; darker cocoa for the close.

Kéré Architecture — Local materials, participation and context are expressed as distinct expertise chapters with people and making photographs.

Olson Kundig — People/place, design context, materiality, studio collaboration and field photography give substance to practice positioning.

Snøhetta — Project grid/list/map choices and discipline/type filters make broad work navigable. Project intro visually inspected; homepage fetch failed, project index succeeded.

David Chipperfield Architects — An index connects projects to news, research, heritage, interviews and publications.

Henning Larsen — Selected work interleaves with research, insight and impact stories. Use project facts and ideas as a second reading layer.

Sundrop

Condensed burgundy uppercase, apricot/coral fields, cream relief, orange drinks, ribbed glass and golden refraction. Repeat the exact orange can and sun emblem; use flavor and gathering rather than generic lifestyle stock.

Ghia — Discovery and build-your-pack offers, strong colored editorial modules, cookbook/hosting story, recipes and newsletter. Sampled browser showed saturated blue and cream bands; some product imagery did not load.

Haus — Pack choice, flavor stories, clear purchase controls, ingredient/serving education, newsletter and shop footer.

Pentire — Bottles, cans and bundles connect to serving instructions, community films, hosting and coastal lifestyle stories.

Seedlip — Flavor discovery moves into serving steps, recipe browsing, origin and journal. Its alcohol-free positioning is not applicable to Sundrop.

Figlia — Compact product choices, community perspective and accessible recipe cards help move from first purchase to use.

Serein

Apricot panels, warm cream, wine roman/italic serif, amber glass with sculptural coral pump, narrow botanical macros, natural adult skin and powder-blue horizontal ritual bands.

Botnia — Bestsellers lead into product/skin/ingredient categories, customized regimen, packaging, batch information, ingredient stories and journal. Visually sampled batch-traceability module; trust comes from concrete product detail.

Furtuna Skin — Hero product and sets connect to process, estate origins, a ritual quiz and journal. Do not transfer clinical results or proprietary process claims.

Monastery — Compact cleanse/moisturize/exfoliate grouping, product texture and making/ingredient story.

Susanne Kaufmann — Ritual builder, sensorial editorial, spa origin, category selection and newsletter create a coherent care journey.

Vintner’s Daughter — A small routine is explained through hero products, usage roles, formulation philosophy, source stories and care education.
