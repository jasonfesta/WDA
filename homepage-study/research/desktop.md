# Desktop reference research

Jason Festa · WDA / Homepage study · 21 October 2026

Five curated references per category. These are design references, not a claim of a popularity ranking. The live-reference register distinguishes inspected interactions from proposed behavior.


## Avela / Soft Form

Terracotta and sea-blue fashion editorial with oxblood leather. Broad cream display letters coexist with delicate serif copy. Let cream product fields alternate with confident terracotta, blue and dark wine sections.

- [Polène](https://eng.polene-paris.com/) — Campaign to category links, compact bestsellers, paired campaign imagery, editorial story, stores, Instagram, newsletter and service footer. Visual sample: full-width paired editorial photographs separated from product grids by generous space.

- [Hereu](https://hereustudio.com/pages/craftmanship) — Material and handwork photography supports the maker story; bags grouped by useful form and carrying category.

- [Neous](https://www.neous.co.uk/) — Sculptural form is connected to everyday function, craft, materials and ownership support. Do not transfer their certifications or repair guarantees.

- [Cuyana](https://cuyana.com/) — Bag families, organizers and carrying systems connect aspirational photography with use; materials and care navigation. Dynamic homepage extraction was partial.

- [Mansur Gavriel](https://www.mansurgavriel.com/) — Campaign-led category entry, distinct bag families and a simple care, resale and newsletter footer.

## Room & Ritual

Rosette pink, deep burgundy, olive velvet, walnut, wool and sculptural orange light. Heavy wordmark plus graceful serif storytelling. Precisely aligned rectangular photography; warm homes with actual inhabitation.

- [Norm Architects](https://normcph.com/) — Project chapters connect residential and hospitality work to furniture, objects and publications. Content reviewed; some images failed to load in the sampled browser viewport.

- [Studio Ashby](https://studioashby.com/projects/) — Regular three-column rectangular interior photographs with simple project captions. Visually inspected: artwork, textiles and lived-in furnishings give each project identity.

- [Studioilse](https://www.studioilse.com/) — Homes, public spaces, things and strategy framed around human use. Transfer the connection between objects and daily rituals.

- [Workstead](https://workstead.com/) — Clear routes for buildings/interiors and lighting; project inquiry and tailored newsletter interests.

- [Roman and Williams](https://romanandwilliams.com/) — Buildings, furniture, lighting, makers and hospitality form an interconnected editorial world.

## Oriel

Monumental cream lowercase type over rust earth architecture, precise daylight, curved honey oak and brass. Lower page uses a disciplined architectural grid and generous image-led case studies; darker cocoa for the close.

- [Kéré Architecture](https://www.kerearchitecture.com/expertise) — Local materials, participation and context are expressed as distinct expertise chapters with people and making photographs.

- [Olson Kundig](https://olsonkundig.com/design-approach/) — People/place, design context, materiality, studio collaboration and field photography give substance to practice positioning.

- [Snøhetta](https://www.snohetta.com/projects/) — Project grid/list/map choices and discipline/type filters make broad work navigable. Project intro visually inspected; homepage fetch failed, project index succeeded.

- [David Chipperfield Architects](https://davidchipperfield.com/) — An index connects projects to news, research, heritage, interviews and publications.

- [Henning Larsen](https://henninglarsen.com/) — Selected work interleaves with research, insight and impact stories. Use project facts and ideas as a second reading layer.

## Sundrop

Condensed burgundy uppercase, apricot/coral fields, cream relief, orange drinks, ribbed glass and golden refraction. Repeat the exact orange can and sun emblem; use flavor and gathering rather than generic lifestyle stock.

- [Ghia](https://drinkghia.com/) — Discovery and build-your-pack offers, strong colored editorial modules, cookbook/hosting story, recipes and newsletter. Sampled browser showed saturated blue and cream bands; some product imagery did not load.

- [Haus](https://drink.haus/) — Pack choice, flavor stories, clear purchase controls, ingredient/serving education, newsletter and shop footer.

- [Pentire](https://pentiredrinks.com/) — Bottles, cans and bundles connect to serving instructions, community films, hosting and coastal lifestyle stories.

- [Seedlip](https://www.seedlipdrinks.com/en-us) — Flavor discovery moves into serving steps, recipe browsing, origin and journal. Its alcohol-free positioning is not applicable to Sundrop.

- [Figlia](https://drinkfiglia.com/) — Compact product choices, community perspective and accessible recipe cards help move from first purchase to use.

## Serein

Apricot panels, warm cream, wine roman/italic serif, amber glass with sculptural coral pump, narrow botanical macros, natural adult skin and powder-blue horizontal ritual bands.

- [Botnia](https://www.botniaskincare.com/) — Bestsellers lead into product/skin/ingredient categories, customized regimen, packaging, batch information, ingredient stories and journal. Visually sampled batch-traceability module; trust comes from concrete product detail.

- [Furtuna Skin](https://www.furtunaskin.com/) — Hero product and sets connect to process, estate origins, a ritual quiz and journal. Do not transfer clinical results or proprietary process claims.

- [Monastery](https://monasterymade.com/) — Compact cleanse/moisturize/exfoliate grouping, product texture and making/ingredient story.

- [Susanne Kaufmann](https://www.susannekaufmann.com/) — Ritual builder, sensorial editorial, spa origin, category selection and newsletter create a coherent care journey.

- [Vintner’s Daughter](https://vintnersdaughter.com/) — A small routine is explained through hero products, usage roles, formulation philosophy, source stories and care education.
