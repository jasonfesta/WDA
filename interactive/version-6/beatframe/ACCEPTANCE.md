# Acceptance and review — Magdalena Bay V2

## Delivery review

The current acceptance target is two concept images and the supporting documentation. Both image files must open correctly and appear in the manifest. Their exact names are `magdalena-bay-desktop-v2.png` and `magdalena-bay-mobile-v2.png`. The artist selection is complete: Magdalena Bay. The current documents must consistently identify the selected Magdalena Bay direction.

Both screens should read as the same distinctive world: pastel atmosphere, chrome portal, tactile scenery and small theatrical cameos. The game is the center of the image. The central lane, portal and middle target share a vertical axis; the outer two targets are evenly distributed. Exactly three lanes and three targets are visible. Neither image uses a persistent sidebar or a giant portrait.

Desktop should show generous scenery on both sides while preserving a quiet note corridor. The pads are labeled D, F and J. Artist, track, score, combo, accuracy, progress and pause are readable without becoming oversized dashboard elements. Environmental highlights should not be confused with playable notes.

Mobile must be an intentional portrait composition rather than a cropped desktop image. All three thumb pads are visible near the bottom with space below them. The artist title is complete, the score strip remains compact and the world retains depth. No device hardware or platform-specific status chrome is required. The screen should still communicate a rhythm game at a smaller review size.

The song title “Image” requires a primary catalog source; the poster requires its named designer credit. The manifest must distinguish official reference imagery from generated proposals. ## Later implementation gate

Functional acceptance applies only after a separate development instruction. It would include recording/chart agreement, stable timing, single-consumption judgments, clear miss recovery, pause integrity, keyboard and touch coverage, and accessible reduced-motion presentation. Use [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) as a review reference.

The art review should record specific requested revisions by region and outcome. Remaining open decisions include chart difficulty, hold-note scope, final font assets, touch dimensions and motion choreography. None blocks review of this completed visual direction.
