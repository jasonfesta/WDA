# Visual direction — surreal 3D dreamscape

## Source and interpretation

The selected reference is the official *Imaginal Disk* poster designed by **Ian Grandjean**, credited by the [Magdalena Bay EU Store](https://magdalenabay.8merch.com/products/imaginal-disk-poster?variant=55080334491990). Its painted science-fiction composition combines strange characters, winged eyes, a disc, star fields and warm/cool atmospheric color. V2 translates that language into a new playable landscape rather than duplicating the poster.

The visual hierarchy is world, centered gameplay and compact identity. Small theatrical cameos create personality at the scene edges. No large face sits behind the notes. The surreal environment determines the palette, layout and character treatment.

## Materials, light and color

Use apricot clouds, periwinkle sky, lavender rock spires and pale peach mist. Liquid chrome provides sharp reflected highlights against matte terrain. The portal is an irregular, sculptural disc edge that frames distant sky. Winged-eye objects and mirrored organic forms repeat the same material language at a smaller scale. Warm coral vegetation provides detail without spreading bright noise across the runway.

The lanes use a quiet smoky-lavender surface. Notes and targets are tactile porcelain-like pieces in peach, ivory and periwinkle, with delicate metallic rims. Their shape and lane position reinforce meaning beyond color. The brightest gameplay accents belong near the targets; background highlights should not become false notes.

## Centered composition

On desktop, the center of the middle lane, portal and middle pad share the same vertical axis. The outer pads are evenly spaced from it. Environmental scenery can vary across the sides, but the gameplay does not drift right to accommodate a portrait. The runway fans outward in perspective and remains visually separate from the terrain.

Mobile is deliberately recomposed. The top identity and metrics occupy a compact upper band, the portal anchors the middle distance, and the three targets span the lower width. Keep breathing room beneath the targets for a safe touch area. There is no phone frame, system-specific status bar or hardware decoration in the concept image.

## Type and visual motion

The BEATFRAME signature uses restrained tracking. MAGDALENA BAY is the principal identity line, with “Image” directly below. Metrics use clean, consistently aligned numerals and a slim progress rule. Pause is small but distinct. Exact font engineering remains a later decision; generated lettering is a visual proposal, not an exported font system.

The scene should feel richly art-directed without becoming a generic neon highway. Future motion belongs to peripheral or distant layers. The hit line, lane geometry and HUD must remain stable through every lighting or portal transition. The two delivered screens establish this composition; they do not prove motion performance or final accessibility.
