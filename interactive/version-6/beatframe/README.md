# BEATFRAME — Magdalena Bay / Imaginal Portal V2

**Selected direction: Magdalena Bay, a surreal 3D dreamscape. Current deliverable: two concept screens and nine specification documents. No new product development is included.**

The user selected Magdalena Bay from the four-artist shortlist. V2 makes the three-lane rhythm game the center of the composition and places it inside a distinctive artist world: peach skies, periwinkle mountains, a liquid-chrome portal, winged-eye sculptures and small theatrical character cameos.

Review [desktop V2](art/magdalena-bay-desktop-v2.png) and [mobile V2](art/magdalena-bay-mobile-v2.png). Both are generated unofficial concept screens. The mobile image is a separate portrait composition with broad thumb pads, not a crop of the desktop. The visible song title, “Image,” is verified against the official artist store's tracklist. Score, combo, accuracy and progress are illustrative; no artist recording or playable chart is delivered.

The visual reference is the official *Imaginal Disk* poster designed by **Ian Grandjean**. Its role is inspiration for the world, not a template to reproduce.
