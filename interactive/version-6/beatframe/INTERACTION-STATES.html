Interaction states — concept contract

Persistent and temporary state

The proposed session separates song selection, chart identity, difficulty, timing adjustment and results from temporary input contacts, hit feedback and world cues. The Magdalena Bay environment is presentation; it cannot alter audio position or note eligibility. A scene transition must never reset a combo, advance a chart or move a target.

Ready: the portal world is visible with MAGDALENA BAY, “Image” and a primary Play action. Sound, difficulty and timing controls are closed by default. A missing recording produces a clear unavailable state rather than a running chart. The present concept package delivers no recording.

Preparation: a short countdown establishes the hit line. Practice presses may illuminate the pads without penalty. The point at which scoring begins is clear. Desktop labels appear once; mobile pads remain direct touch targets without keyboard letters.

Playing: the three lanes, target centers and top metrics stay fixed. Each note can receive one judgment. Perfect, Good and Miss feedback appears briefly near the hit line, with enough distinction to understand recovery. The portal and winged-eye orbits may respond to phrase cues outside the note corridor.

Paused: a compact temporary layer contains Resume, Restart and settings. In a future build, sound and chart time stop together, held input clears and resume includes a return countdown. The world remains visible behind the layer. Closing settings restores the same session rather than beginning again.

Interruptions and optional inputs

A hidden tab, lost focus or interrupted audio device should enter a recoverable pause. An orientation change preserves position and recomposes the centered lanes before resuming. Touch cancellation clears the corresponding lane contact. If holds are later approved, the recovery policy must be explicit rather than silently counting a broken hold as success.

A possible local-file practice mode would keep the last valid selection after an invalid audio or image choice. Artwork selection must not silently change audio, chart or difficulty. It should provide an obvious return to the curated Magdalena Bay study. This is an optional future flow, not a delivered control in these stills.

Results and access

Results follow the final resolved judgment. Retry starts a clean session; Back returns to the ready state. Accuracy, best combo and score should exist as readable text in any future build, not only rendered pixels. Controls need meaningful names, visible focus and sufficient separation. Settings fields should receive typed input without also firing gameplay shortcuts.

The delivered images depict Playing only. Ready, countdown, pause, errors and results are specified here for a later design pass. No state logic has been implemented for this concept.
