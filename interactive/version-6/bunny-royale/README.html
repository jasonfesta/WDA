Bunny Royale

Concept pack · 24 October 2026 · First-level art, specification and future production plan.

Choose an expressive rabbit fighter, load an oversized blaster and clear a bright suburban arena. The concept combines the spatial readability and stylized rendering of Fortnite with chaotic rabbit comedy. It is a level-based single-player proposal; the name does not imply a completed multiplayer battle royale.

Jason approved Pip, Bruno and Volt. Their shapes, equipment and palettes are retained. The original resort environment was rejected; the current suburban environment is revision 2 and remains for review. No new gameplay development is included in this package.

Start with the art review, then read the documents in this order:

  1. Product: promise, audience, boundaries and decisions.
  2. Experience: first session and first-level sequence.
  3. Visual direction: world, character and interface rules.
  4. Interaction states: controls, HUD, failures and accessibility.
  5. Content and assets: existing art and remaining production inventory.
  6. Technical plan: future architecture and feasibility questions.
  7. Acceptance: review criteria and later implementation checks.
  8. Sources: references, approvals and provenance.

The current manifest contains 27 raster assets. The selected assets and exact prompts are included in this folder. Turnarounds are modeling references; rigs, geometry, animation, audio and a playable game still require production. This document set makes those next decisions concrete without starting them.
