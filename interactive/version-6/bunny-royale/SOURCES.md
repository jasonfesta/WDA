# Bunny Royale — Sources and decision record

## Primary reference

- [Fortnite, Epic Games](https://www.fortnite.com/) — visual and genre precedent. Reviewed 24 October 2026. The intended borrowing is stylized third-person spatial readability and edge HUD hierarchy. Bunny Royale is an unofficial original concept, not an Epic product or a claim of affiliation.

## Local source of truth

- [Art direction](art-direction.txt), [asset manifest](manifest.json) and [art README](README.md) distinguish current assets from superseded studies.
- Decision record: the project is greenlit for art; Pip, Bruno and Volt are approved. The first environment was rejected and a Fortnite-like suburban revision requested. This publication contains concepts, art and specifications only.

The current visuals were generated with the ImageGen. Prompts are saved beside the assets. Generated gameplay views illustrate intended design; they are not captures of working gameplay. Mechanics, level pacing and future technical structure in this pack are design proposals rather than facts supplied by the reference games.
