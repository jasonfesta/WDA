BUNNY ROYALE — FIRST LEVEL ART DIRECTION
Project and character direction approved by Jason, 24 October 2026. Revised environment and UI remain for review. Image art package only; no playable game implementation. Deliver actual raster art plus a local review gallery. Turnarounds are rendered character reference sheets, not rigged 3D meshes.

STYLE
Fortnite-like polished stylized 3D game rendering: broad readable silhouettes, chamfered forms, saturated sunny natural colors, chunky foliage, tactile painted metal, clean expressive faces, dramatic but friendly animation-ready proportions. Rabbids-inspired comic rabbit energy, with our own character designs. No existing Fortnite or Rabbids named characters. No realistic blood. No gritty military realism. Rich actual scene imagery; not flat diagrams posing as art.

THREE PLAYABLE CHARACTERS — keep names, palettes, body proportions and weapons consistent
PIP / SCOUT: ivory-white fur; athletic small rabbit with oversized upright ears and one bent tip; broad expressive eyes, protruding paired buck teeth, mischievous grin. Teal padded chest armor, orange scarf and orange knee guards, dark navy undersuit, teal/orange boots, small carrot-shaped backpack battery. Weapon: CARROT CARBINE, chunky teal/orange compact two-hand blaster with a translucent orange cylindrical magazine; shoots bright orange carrot-shaped energy bolts. Ability: double hop. Default hero for menus and gameplay.
BRUNO / HEAVY: warm sand-colored fur; broad stocky muscular rabbit, thick low ears, enormous cheeky grin. Olive-green rounded heavy chest and shoulder armor, mustard-yellow accents, dark brown undersuit, heavy boots, large round orange canister backpack. Weapon: SEED THUMPER, short olive/mustard drum cannon with an oversized round muzzle and visible seed-pod drum; shoots bouncing orange seed bombs. Ability: ground slam.
VOLT / TECH: pale lavender fur; tall slim rabbit, long swept-back ears, cobalt blue goggles pushed onto forehead, delighted slightly unhinged expression. Cobalt and violet segmented light armor, hot-pink illuminated seams, pale gray undersuit, small twin-disc backpack. Weapon: ARC FORK, compact cobalt two-pronged energy blaster with pink conductor coils; shoots branching pink electrical arcs. Ability: decoy drone.

LEVEL 01 — CARROT COVE / ENVIRONMENT V2 CORRECTION
Jason rejected the resort-like environment and explicitly said it must look just like Fortnite. The characters are approved and stay unchanged. Use env-fortnite-suburb-v2.png as the canonical environment reference, derived from a real Fortnite gameplay screenshot. Classic stylized suburban combat map: blue wooden-siding houses, red-brick market, asphalt road, parked pickup truck and yellow car, low wood fences, wooden cover crates, bright broad grass, chunky deciduous trees and pines, vivid green hills with simple cliffs, blue-violet sky. Clean angular game meshes, simplified matte game textures, no photoreal microdetail. Three encounter spaces: neighborhood arrival, market intersection, service yard. Rooftops, back gardens, fences and bounce pads provide flanking paths. Only subtle carrot branding. NO Mediterranean village, resort, orange tile roofs, cobbled piazza, fantasy carrot tower or miniature diorama. All previous env-carrot-cove-master/map/modular-props images are superseded studies and must not appear as current art.

FIRST LEVEL ENEMIES
Use inexpensive visual variants of the same rabbit world: white-fur orange-vest carrot raider (ranged grunt), white-fur yellow-helmet hopper (melee), large cream-fur blue-armored foreman boss with orange seed drum. All comic, expressive and visually distinguishable from teal/olive/cobalt playable cast.

UI / BRAND
BUNNY ROYALE exact wordmark. Strong condensed playful chunky white 3D letters with midnight-navy outline and subtle orange accent, simple ear silhouette integrated into mark. White compact typography, teal/orange highlights. Main menus use the entire environment behind characters. No big permanent sidebar over active world. Character selection is a separate full-screen stage with three portraits along the bottom, selected character large center, small floating loadout summary at right edge. Combat HUD only at corners/edges: health and shield bottom left; ammo/3 inventory slots bottom right; wave objective top center; crosshair center. Pause/settings are deliberate modal states, not persistent editor panels. Final UI deliverables are raster design screens, not coded functional game menus.
