Track Atlas

Specification only · Current visual direction: watercolor V2 · One product, three modes.

Track Atlas turns a listening session into an authored watercolor composition. Ink Score paints a musical landscape, Kinetic Poster integrates a track title with expressive brushwork, and Listening Diary collects painted memories of moments the listener chooses. All three share one track, one playhead, and the same compact controls. The active canvas stays open; there is no persistent sidebar.

The current deliverable is a concept and art package. The review gallery contains three desktop screens, three separately composed mobile screens, and a shared control sheet. It does not analyze or play audio. “Quiet Current,” its 96 BPM, and the displayed timestamps are fictional demonstration metadata.

No implementation is authorized or included in this package. The next decision is whether the proposed experience and visual rules are strong enough to support a separately approved prototype.
