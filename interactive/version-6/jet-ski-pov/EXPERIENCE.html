Proposed ride sequence

First minute

The opening view is already in first person. The craft floats in a protected bay, with soft water movement beneath it and a distant landmark giving orientation. A small Ride action starts the session. There is no cinematic fly-through that delays control or changes the camera's relationship to the craft.

During the first fifteen seconds, the rider accelerates through two broad buoy gates. The route is visible through objects in the world, not a large map. A small steering hint can appear near the grips and fade once a useful turn is made. The dashboard communicates speed with an easy-to-read number and a restrained sense of motor load.

The next passage crosses a gentle wake. The craft lifts and settles, teaching water contact before asking for a trick. A contextual preload hint appears near the relevant input only while it can be used. At the first broad ramp, the rider may simply jump and land. A clean landing is already satisfying and earns a short score stamp.

Three-zone course

The sheltered bay provides room to learn. The slalom channel introduces a choice between a wider controlled line and a shorter sequence requiring sharper steering. The open-water field offers larger crests and one final authored jump. A recognizable landmark persists across zones, preventing disorientation when the rider turns away from the route.

Risk grows through decisions rather than constant speed inflation. A rider can take an easier jump and bank a small chain, or attempt an aerial action and risk losing unbanked points. The final jump should feel like a culmination of earlier learning, not a surprise mechanical test.

Landing and recovery

A clean landing creates a short spray burst and a clear, restrained result. A rough landing reduces speed and breaks fluidity but keeps the run going. A wipeout uses a brief visual transition and returns to the previous reliable marker. Recovery should be fast enough to encourage another attempt and predictable enough to preserve trust.

The camera maintains a broadly stable horizon. The craft may move beneath the rider, but large compulsory rolls are not part of the default experience. Optional camera shake must not become the only way to perceive landing force.

Finish and free ride

The course ends with time, clean landings, best chain, and Retry. Free ride instead offers an unhurried exit summary. Pause is always reachable. The experience remains usable without music, without look-around, and without optional tricks. These are proposed sequences for review; the package contains no simulated ride or recorded gameplay.
