Sources and provenance

Reviewed 24 October 2026, local time. Current product behavior is proposed; source references establish precedents or technical capabilities, not proof that Track Atlas is implemented.

Project evidence

  • Current watercolor gallery, V2 art direction, and manifest with exact prompts: seven generated raster studies produced for this concept. The metadata “Quiet Current / 96 BPM” is fictional.

Visual precedents

  • DIA — A-Trak Live Visuals: a typographic identity developed into varied artwork and live visuals. Supports the idea of one expressive type system; it is not the source of the new watercolor illustration.
  • Dear Data — data postcards: Giorgia Lupi and Stefanie Posavec's analog data-drawing project. Informs personal annotation and authored visual records. Track Atlas does not reproduce its postcard designs.

Technical and accessibility foundations

  • W3C Web Audio API: audio-processing and analyser reference. The retrieved 1.1 document is a Working Draft; its presence does not establish browser compatibility or an emotion-recognition feature.
  • W3C Understanding Animation from Interactions: informs the decision to offer motion reduction and direct control. It is guidance, not an accessibility audit of the generated screens.

All creative mappings, experience targets, scope choices and proposed validation gates in this folder are design decisions. They should not be attributed to the reference authors. Production audio, editable paint assets and implementation measurements remain future work.
