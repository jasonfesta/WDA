# Product

**Status: proposed product specification; no working audio experience has been built.**

## Promise

Track Atlas gives people a way to make something from listening. Instead of only watching an automatic visualizer, the visitor can shape a painted phrase, emphasize a word, or record a personal memory at a particular moment. The result should feel recognizably connected to the track and visibly influenced by the listener.

The primary audience is a curious listener with little technical knowledge. A secondary audience is a designer, musician or visual artist exploring how sound can inform composition. Neither audience should need to understand frequency bins before making a compelling image. The first useful outcome is a saved still, not a finished music video or an audio production session.

## One product, three expressions

| Mode | Creative action | Intended artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink Score | Pull a painted current and place rhythmic marks | A landscape shaped around a musical phrase |
| Kinetic Poster | Arrange title lettering and watercolor forms | An expressive typographic poster |
| Listening Diary | Mark a moment and add a short personal annotation | A sequence of illustrated listening memories |

Switching mode preserves track position, playback state, loop region and session identity. Each mode retains its own composition, so trying another view does not erase work. An annotation's timestamp is shared; its placement can differ between visual modes.

## Meaningful boundaries

For the first proposed release, “arrangement” means visual arrangement. Moving a river or letterform does not reorder the music. Audio remixing, stem separation, instrument recognition, automatic lyric transcription, streaming-service integration and live microphone input are outside this concept's initial scope.

The system may calculate amplitude and frequency-derived features, and may estimate rhythmic events. A visible distinction must remain between measured values, estimates, creative visual mappings and user-authored meaning. A loud passage is not evidence that a listener is excited. Words such as “lift,” “calm” and “space” are optional descriptions chosen by a person, not emotional diagnoses from audio.

## Minimum worthwhile experience

Provide one commissioned or licensed demonstration track, local-file selection, three visual modes, play/pause, seeking, a loop region, undo, a small mapping explanation and still export. Save a lightweight composition record so a person can continue later after reconnecting their local audio. Account creation and public sharing are unnecessary for this first experience.

Success means a new visitor can explain what changed because of the music, what changed because of their own action, and how to keep the result. Proposed review targets are a first deliberate visual edit within one minute and an understandable save flow within three minutes. These are goals to test, not measured outcomes. A beautiful unattended animation alone does not satisfy the product promise.
