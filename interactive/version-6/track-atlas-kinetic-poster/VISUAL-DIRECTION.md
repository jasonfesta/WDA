# Visual direction

**Current direction: watercolor V2. The [seven generated studies](manifest.json) establish appearance, not production-ready layers.**

## An illustrated listening surface

The image should read as a watercolor illustration before it reads as a visualizer. Pigment has translucent overlaps, pooled edges, dry-brush interruptions and small blooms. Paper is a supporting material, not a substitute for painted subject matter. A generic waveform placed on textured beige does not meet this direction.

Use a restrained family of deep indigo, muted teal, terracotta, golden ochre and softened moss on warm paper. Indigo gives structure, terracotta supplies warmth, and ochre opens brighter areas. Thin black ink marks supply rhythmic detail. Orange is also the selection and playhead accent, so active controls need a shape or label in addition to color.

The illustration should leave quiet regions around controls and preserve visible paper between major forms. Avoid filling every gap with particles, flowers or decorative marks. The current studies are deliberately rich; production art must still allow quieter passages and meaningful differences in density.

## Three composition grammars

**Ink Score:** a current travels through a landscape of overlapping washes. Long gestures suggest sustained phrases, and small marks provide rhythm. The desktop image extends horizontally; the portrait version carries the eye down a winding river. Maintain identifiable foreground, middle distance and open sky without making a literal map of the audio.

**Kinetic Poster:** the title is painted into the image. It should remain readable as “Quiet Current” in the demonstration composition. Letterforms can interact with botanical strokes and broad washes, but essential words must not be cropped or continuously deformed beyond recognition. The precise UI font and expressive artwork lettering have separate jobs.

**Listening Diary:** several distinct vignettes form a sequence of remembered moments. A pool, flowers and a horizon provide the current visual vocabulary. Fine connecting lines, timestamps and handwritten annotations make the sequence personal. New entries should create intentional spacing rather than an endless pile of decorative cards.

## The control layer

Use small, clear Inter-style typography, thin rules and direct labels. The product name and file actions occupy the top edge; mode choices and transport form a shallow lower area. This is an edge control system, with no persistent vertical sidebar over the canvas. Detailed editing belongs in a temporary focused view that is easy to close.

Generated text and spacing are references, not exact design tokens. A later interface design pass must establish font files, sizes, hit areas, contrast and focus treatments. Watercolor texture must never reduce the legibility of playback or error messages.

## Motion character

Favor slow pigment reveals, gentle emphasis and occasional rhythmic marks. Avoid camera travel, relentless zooming or a full-scene pulse on every beat. The visitor can disable decorative motion while retaining sound and precise seeking. That choice follows the intent of [W3C guidance on interaction-triggered animation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html); the package makes no accessibility conformance claim.
