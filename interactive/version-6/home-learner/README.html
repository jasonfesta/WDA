Home Learner — concept pack

Working title · Android V2 · 25 October 2026 · concept and raster art only.

Home Learner is an Android app and mobile companion for teaching from life at home through a head camera, webcam or phone. Demonstrate a bounded lesson, label what matters, review observations, prepare a model version, and test an unseen situation. The current illustration uses “Pack my bag”; it does not imply general task learning or robotic execution.

Start with Product, then Experience. The remaining briefs cover visual direction, interaction states, content and assets, technical feasibility, acceptance and sources.

Android V2 hero · Setup · Capture · Review · Art manifest.

No product code, dependency setup, capture, training, cloud service or worker network is included. The technical plan describes possible future work and decisions; it is not authorization to implement.

V2 uses warm chalk, charcoal and restrained citron with Android system treatment and compact Material 3-inspired controls. Head cam remains the source throughout the new lesson sequence.
