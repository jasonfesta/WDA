Android V2 visual direction

A home teaching instrument

Home Learner is now explicitly an Android app. V2 uses warm chalk, stone, deep charcoal and a restrained citron accent. Photography supplies the human warmth; controls provide a quiet precise structure. The home lesson remains Pack my bag, with a navy backpack, mustard notebook, sage bottle and white charger on an oak table.

The physical-device hero shows a person wearing a practical head camera while an Android phone acts as companion and controller. Its generic graphite handset has a small circular punch-hole and slim bezels. It is not a claim about a particular manufacturer's hardware or a functioning headcam connection.

Surface, type and rhythm

Use chalk near #EEECE4 for setup and review, charcoal near #252720 for primary text and capture controls, and citron near #D8EE83 for the selected action. Tonal stone dividers and rows carry secondary structure. Citron is a focus accent, not a background for every component.

A precise Roboto-like sans should establish a small set of headline, title, body and label roles. Setup uses a confident lesson heading; capture reduces typography to a compact rail; review gives priority to evidence and corrections. Spacing and alignment do more work than card borders. Material 3 informs roles, touch behavior and tonal grouping while the visual identity remains custom.

Three composed states

Setup is a dedicated screen with a lesson preview, a single source selector and flat settings rows. Head cam is selected; Webcam and Phone remain alternatives. The Start lesson action is a medium-sized button aligned within the composition.

Capture puts the live image edge to edge. A compact top rail identifies Head cam, Visual cues and recording status. A shallow charcoal dock holds Mark step, Pause and Finish. The middle of the scene stays unobstructed. A short voice label appears near the bottom of the camera, not in a conversation panel.

Review is a focused evidence editor: a large Head cam preview, narrow filmstrip, flat observation rows and one expanded correction. Dataset 03 and Model 02 remain visibly separate. Head cam is the source throughout this sequence; review never silently changes to Webcam.

Android behavior and art status

Account for system status, cutout and navigation areas. Compact controls still need 48 dp touch targets, font scaling and accessible labels. The status and gesture treatment in the art is one Android configuration; three-button navigation also requires future validation.
