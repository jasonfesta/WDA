The home teaching experience

First session

The Android companion opens with a proposed lesson, Pack my bag, and a preview of the capture area. Choose Head cam, Webcam or Phone. These are alternative perspectives, not three feeds that must all run. A source that cannot connect offers import of a previously recorded clip. A head camera may require a supported bridge or file transfer; the concept must not imply universal pairing.

Before recording, name the outcome and choose an appropriate model capability. Confirm where the session will be saved, whether voice labels are wanted, and whether anyone else may enter the frame. Review capture area shows what will be included and lets the person exclude a region or move the camera. This is a short preparation step, not a persistent settings panel.

Demonstrate and mark

Start lesson enters a large live view with a visible source, timer and recording state. The user places the notebook in the bag and says “This is my notebook.” A Mark step action bookmarks the moment. Voice narration is evidence until the person approves its label; automatic transcription, if available, is identified as a draft.

Pause is available throughout. Head-camera users can keep the phone nearby as a controller; a future supported remote button could mark moments, but it is not assumed in the first slice. Source switching ends or pauses the current segment so perspectives and timestamps remain interpretable.

Review, teach and test

Finish opens a short filmstrip. Review each proposed observation against its clip. Confirm, edit, split or discard it. The charger example is intentionally unresolved: it is visible on the table, so “packed” is not established. The correction remains linked to that segment.

Saving the review creates a dataset version. Preparing a model version is a separate action with explicit capability, data selection and processing location. The current usable model remains available during preparation or failure.

Test current model starts a new session held outside training. Move the bag, change the order, or leave out an item. Show proposed observations and uncertainty without silently relabeling them as truth. Corrections can join the next dataset version only after review.

A useful session ends with three answers: what the person taught, what the model actually used, and what still failed. A short evidence-based recap is more valuable than a celebratory accuracy number.

Continuity in the current study

Setup, capture and review all use Head cam for Lesson 01. Webcam and Phone are available alternatives, not unexplained source changes. Android system Back returns through the lesson flow without silently discarding a recording; an active session needs an explicit pause/finish choice. The capture canvas stays clear while source, model and recording state remain legible at the edge.
