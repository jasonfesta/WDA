# Product intent

Home Learner makes personal model teaching understandable through a home lesson. A person can demonstrate a routine using a head camera, set a webcam beside a table, or point their phone. The Android companion selects the source, guides the lesson, marks meaningful moments and supports correction. The central value is a useful, inspectable teaching record that can support an appropriately scoped model.

## The teaching contract

Each lesson starts with an outcome the selected model can represent. “Recognize these three objects” fits a small visual classifier. “Find likely moments when these objects appear” may combine that classifier with timestamped evidence. “Understand every step of packing a bag” requires a separate temporal capability and evaluation. “Pack my bag for me” is outside this concept. The product must never collapse these outcomes into one Train button.

The illustrated lesson, Pack my bag, contains Notebook, Bottle and Charger. A person marks the steps and narrates short labels. Suggested observations remain drafts until reviewed. An object being visible does not prove it was packed; a review card deliberately shows this distinction. The user can correct the observation to “charger remains on table” and retain the evidence.

## Who it is for

The first user is a curious person collecting a small lesson in their own home, not a machine-learning operator. A second useful role is the reviewer who helps label a session with permission. A future device contributor can offer capture or compute resources without gaining control over unrelated home media.

The product should feel like teaching a patient apprentice: demonstrate, check, improve. It should also make uncertainty easy to see. Confidence charts are secondary to the actual clip, label and reason a result needs review.

## Scope and boundaries

The first conceptual slice is one home routine, one active source, optional voice notes, manual labels, a bounded observation model and an unseen test session. Sound classification, transcription and temporal observation models are separate selectable capabilities. Device workers, cloud processing and larger-model adaptation are later choices.

Success is understanding what was captured, what was approved as data, which model used it, and what improved on an unseen example. No claim is made that more footage automatically creates a capable personal agent. The owner decides the capture area, participants, retention and processing location before starting.


## Android product direction

Android is the target platform. The interface should feel purpose-built for a handheld teaching tool: edge-to-edge camera, a compact source/model rail and a shallow action dock. Warm stone, charcoal and a restrained citron accent replace the earlier blue study. Material 3 informs hierarchy and controls while the photographic lesson remains the focus.
