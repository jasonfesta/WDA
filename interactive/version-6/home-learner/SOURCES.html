Sources and provenance

Research reviewed 24 October 2026; Android direction updated 25 October 2026. These sources establish specific precedents and platform constraints; the Home Learner experience is a new proposal.

  • Google Creative Lab — Teachable Machine: precedent for gathering examples, training bounded classes and testing. It is not evidence of arbitrary routine understanding.
  • Google Codelabs — TensorFlow.js transfer learning: demonstrates frozen MobileNet features and a learned classifier head. This supports the narrow local-learning option.
  • Google AI Edge — image classification: category outputs from images/video frames. Temporal completion and task execution are separate proposed capabilities.
  • MDN — getUserMedia: permissioned media input, secure-context requirements and unavailable/denied input behavior. It does not establish compatibility with every head camera.

New images were generated with the ImageGen. They are concept imagery, not actual captures of a working product or real training results. Art manifest links the exact saved files and prompts.

Assumptions requiring future evidence: supported headcam transport, practical model size on chosen phones, temporal observation quality, automatic transcription quality, resource savings from worker devices, and any local/cloud deployment choice. No source here is an endorsement, partnership or permission to reuse proprietary product art as original work.

Android V2 grounding:

  • Android Developers — system bars: edge-to-edge content, display cutouts, contrast and navigation insets.
  • Android Developers — accessibility: minimum 48 dp targets and alternatives to gesture-only actions.
  • Android Developers — Material 3 theming: semantic color/type/shape roles with custom brand expression. The stone/charcoal/citron palette and layouts are original design choices.

The current art manifest identifies the four selected Android studies.
