# Graphic direction

## A poster that can be performed

Type Rave should look like graphic design before it moves. Begin with a strong static phrase, controlled line breaks, a purposeful scale relationship, and margins that can survive animation. Motion should reveal alternative compositions already latent in that arrangement. If a frozen frame looks like arbitrary letters scattered by noise, the system has lost its direction.

The [DIA A-Trak project](https://dia.tv/project/a-trak/) shows a typographic identity extended through a defined visual vocabulary. Borrow the discipline of parameters, not the exact wordmark or artwork. The local reference image remains credited to DIA and its publisher. It is not presented as a new Type Rave design.

## Type and palette

Use Inter for small interface labels and evaluate a clear, open-licensed display face for the performed phrase. Inter itself is a viable initial candidate; its [official source](https://rsms.me/inter/) identifies the font and license. The final choice should support the intended weight and width range without destroying counters at extreme settings.

The core palette is warm paper and near-black, with one acidic accent. A second palette may invert the relationship for performance, but the concept should not rely on a rainbow of unrelated effects. Accent color signals a selected word, a beat marker, or a live input, not every control at once.

## Three behavior studies

Breathe expands the spacing of whole words while baselines remain stable. At rest it reads as a balanced poster. Stack moves complete lines into blocks, sometimes revealing a band of accent color between them. It should retain visible rhythm and alignment even at its most compact. Elastic stretches a selected word or word group, then releases it into a readable shape. Adjacent text anchors the viewer while the selected group moves.

For each behavior, art should show rest, moderate energy, peak, and frozen frames. These states will reveal whether the system creates a coherent range rather than one attractive accident.

## Screen composition

The phrase owns almost the entire canvas. A shallow bottom strip holds phrase, behavior, intensity, play, freeze, and save. Mapping appears as a temporary small surface near the action. Selection handles appear only when useful. The interface never grows into a persistent sidebar.

Phone composition needs larger direct controls and deliberate wrapping; it cannot simply shrink the desktop poster. Reduced motion should preserve weight and spacing changes while removing dramatic displacement. No camera movement is needed. The current pack specifies this direction and preserves a reference image; it does not deliver new motion graphics or a working typography renderer.
