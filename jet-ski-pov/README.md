# Jet Ski POV — ocean-riding concept

**Status: concept and specification only. No simulation is being developed.**

Jet Ski POV preserves the selected direction: first-person handlebars, open water, a readable horizon, responsive carving, and the satisfaction of jumping and landing. It is a standalone ocean experience, with no rhythm lanes or requirement to steer on musical beats.

The [reference photograph](references/jet-ski-pov-photograph.jpg) is an existing published image used for framing and scale. It is not generated game art and not a browser prototype. The proposed setting uses an original coastal course instead of copying the reference skyline or branded craft.

The pack separates [Product](PRODUCT.md), [Experience](EXPERIENCE.md), [Visual direction](VISUAL-DIRECTION.md), [Interaction states](INTERACTION-STATES.md), [Content and assets](CONTENT-ASSETS.md), [Technical plan](TECHNICAL-PLAN.md), and [Acceptance](ACCEPTANCE.md). [Sources](SOURCES.md) records the photograph and primary technical references.

The first proposed session is a short authored run through a sheltered bay, slalom channel, and open jump field, with free ride on the same water. The design prioritizes an unobstructed forward view and forgiving recovery. Duration, water scale, final craft styling, trick complexity, and measured performance remain open decisions. This pack makes those choices reviewable; it does not imply that a renderer, physics system, level, or audio engine has been built.
