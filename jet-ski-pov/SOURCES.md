# Sources and evidence

- [Uptown Jet Skis](https://www.uptownjetskis.com/): retained publisher source for the [point-of-view photograph](references/jet-ski-pov-photograph.jpg). This is existing photography used to study handlebars, water scale, and horizon placement. The retained record does not identify an individual photographer. It is not a simulation screen.
- [Three.js ocean example](https://threejs.org/examples/webgl_shaders_ocean.html): primary rendering reference. Its scope is the displayed water example, not the proposed craft physics or course.
- [Three.js documentation](https://threejs.org/docs/): primary API reference for future rendering, camera, asset, and resource planning.
- [Pointer Events Level 3](https://www.w3.org/TR/pointerevents3/): primary input-standard reference for later mouse, pen, and touch planning.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): reference for future accessibility review.

This expanded pack preserves the selected direction while separating product, experience, visuals, states, content, technical options, and acceptance.

Course zones, trick vocabulary, reset rules, camera defaults, asset requirements, and performance targets are creative or engineering proposals. They are not claims made by the cited sources. No new game art, code, simulation, or benchmark was produced for this pack.
