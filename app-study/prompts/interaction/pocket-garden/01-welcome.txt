ENTRY: First launch
STATE: Pocket Garden brand. The original illustrated gardener among olive foliage and peach flowers fills the top 60%, extending fully to both side edges and top, fading into warm cream. Headline "Your little garden starts here." Body "Start with one plant. Learn its little rhythms." Main "Add my first plant". Secondary "Explore a sample garden". No early-access badge, no spot-saved claim. No tabs.
DISPLAY: Brand, headings, body copy, background texture, decorative vignettes and dividers are noninteractive. Read meaningful copy in document order; decorative artwork uses empty alt. OS chrome is intentionally omitted from exports; production safe-area insets belong to the app shell. Step markers communicate progress and are not buttons. Numeric totals derive from saved data or the explicitly described draft. Any exceptions appear in the control ledger.
AMBIENT: 4-second leaf-and-light introduction; one optional blink, then hold.
ACTIONS: The primary start CTA deepens its terracotta tint on hover/press. No plant grows or becomes saved until setup succeeds.
SCROLL/RETURN: One vertical scroll region. Preserve the airy spacing; at small heights or large text allow scrolling instead of scaling text down. Keep the bottom destination navigation above the safe area when present, and add content inset so it covers nothing. Keyboard opening scrolls the focused field into view; closing it restores the prior position. No horizontal paging gesture changes destination. Motion layer: No parallax; artwork and introduction scroll together. Returning does not replay. Use normal document scroll; never hijack scrolling or move the foreground type with a background effect. Decorative layers stay inside their illustration region. Suspend them when offscreen, document-hidden, an editor has keyboard focus, or a modal covers the screen. Return to the same phase without catch-up bursts.

effectiveReducedMotion = system preference OR saved app preference. When true, use a static poster, freeze shader time and 3D poses, remove parallax, and apply state changes immediately. Functional pointer-following remains direct, with no inertial or settling animation. Text and status feedback remain complete.
