ENTRY: Find my rhythm
STATE: Brand Afterglow; back arrow; 2 / 2. Headline "When does your evening slow down?" Readout "10:45 pm" and subtitle "Your wind-down time". Frosted picker hours 10, minutes 45, pm; adjacent muted values. Tiny amber crescent. Optional switch "Remind me 15 minutes before" OFF, helper "You can turn this on later." Main CTA "Set my evening". No tabs. Body: Make a little room for a calmer evening. No coffee claim.
DISPLAY: Brand, headings, body copy, background texture, decorative vignettes and dividers are noninteractive. Read meaningful copy in document order; decorative artwork uses empty alt. OS chrome is intentionally omitted from exports; production safe-area insets belong to the app shell. Step markers communicate progress and are not buttons. Numeric totals derive from saved data or the explicitly described draft. Any exceptions appear in the control ledger.
AMBIENT: Hold the background completely still while choosing a time.
ACTIONS: Picker selection settles within 180 ms after direct input; 10:45 pm and the reminder preview update from one draft value. Switch thumb takes 140 ms only after the state is permitted.
SCROLL/RETURN: One vertical scroll region. Preserve the airy spacing; at small heights or large text allow scrolling instead of scaling text down. Keep the bottom destination navigation above the safe area when present, and add content inset so it covers nothing. Keyboard opening scrolls the focused field into view; closing it restores the prior position. No horizontal paging gesture changes destination. Motion layer: Keyboard/picker cannot be covered by the CTA. Returning from permission keeps the draft and focus. Use normal document scroll; never hijack scrolling or move the foreground type with a background effect. Decorative layers stay inside their illustration region. Suspend them when offscreen, document-hidden, an editor has keyboard focus, or a modal covers the screen. Return to the same phase without catch-up bursts.

effectiveReducedMotion = system preference OR saved app preference. When true, use a static poster, freeze shader time and 3D poses, remove parallax, and apply state changes immediately. Functional pointer-following remains direct, with no inertial or settling animation. Text and status feedback remain complete.
