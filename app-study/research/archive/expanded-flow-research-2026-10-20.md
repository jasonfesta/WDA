## Mobile research and interpretation

- [Cozi features](https://www.cozi.com/feature-overview/): household coordination, shared plans and lists → KindredNote setup, handovers and household views.
- [Headspace sleep](https://www.headspace.com/content/categories/sleep): ritual and sound-library/player patterns → Emberrest evening setup, ritual library, player and weekly rhythm.
- [Planta](https://getplanta.com/): plant records and contextual care → Spriglet plant/light setup, care checks and observation journal.
- [I Love Hue](https://zutgames.com/i-love-hue.php): calm colour arrangement → Florivo tutorial, puzzle, completion and collections. Original art and mechanics presentation; no copied levels.
- [Apple Weather guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/check-the-weather-iph1ac0b35f/26/ios/26): current, hourly and saved locations → Dayglint forecast, places and preferences.
- [Structured](https://structured.app/): timeline and calendar/planning patterns → Evenfall day, plan editor, week and places.
- [Apple onboarding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding) and [privacy](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy/): optional setup and contextual permission requests. The dynamic documentation was supplemented by official indexed excerpts; no complete visual audit claimed.

Ten screens means four onboarding, four core views, account/profile and settings. Permission requests are not depicted as already approved. Calendar dates and counts are specified in the prompts. Thursday 18 June refers to 2026.
