## Mobile and interaction research

| App or family | Primary reference | Design decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home Note | [Cozi feature overview](https://www.cozi.com/feature-overview/) | Household setup, shared day, handover notes and people |
| Afterglow | [Headspace sleep](https://www.headspace.com/content/categories/sleep) | Evening routine, ritual detail, sound player and rhythm preferences |
| Pocket Garden | [Planta](https://getplanta.com/) | Plant collection, plant detail, care queue and notes; proposed post-launch screens |
| Petal | [I Love Hue](https://zutgames.com/i-love-hue.php) | Distinct play, hint, pause, completion and library states |
| Sol | [Apple Weather guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/check-the-weather-iph1ac0b35f/26/ios/26) | Current conditions, hourly detail, locations and settings |
| Daybook | [Structured](https://structured.app/) | Timeline, plan creation, saved plan, week and places |
| Navigation | [W3C tabs pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tabs/) | Selected tab must correspond to its panel; keyboard focus is distinct |
| Selection | [W3C listbox pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/listbox/) | Explicit selection, consistent multi-selection and count feedback |

The [page and state plan](mobile-state-plan-2026-10-20.md) turns this research into concrete screens. The project rules define acceptance checks.
